Privacy policy

srv1-backup · last updated 20 October 2026

Who this applies to

srv1-backup is a personal tool. Its only user is the owner of the server it backs up, who is also the owner of the Google account it connects to.

What data the application accesses

  • Files in the owner's own Google Drive, through the Google Drive API, to store and manage the encrypted backup files in a dedicated backup folder.
  • The files on the owner's own server that are included in the backup.

How the data is used

Data is used only to create, verify and restore backups. Backups are encrypted on the server before they are uploaded, with a password that only the owner holds.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared with or transferred to any third party. Nothing is sent anywhere other than the owner's own Google Drive. The application has no analytics, advertising or tracking.

Storage and deletion

Backups are kept in the owner's Google Drive according to the owner's retention settings and can be deleted by the owner at any time. Access can be revoked at any time in the Google Account settings under "Third-party apps & services".

Google API Services

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
